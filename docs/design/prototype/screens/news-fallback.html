AI demand pushes memory makers to the limit

AI 依原文改寫，約 280 字｜原文：Semiconductor Engineering

Memory makers are racing to ramp up production of high-bandwidth memory as demand from AI data centers keeps climbing. Analysts say the biggest supply bottleneck is no longer the chips themselves but the advanced packaging needed to stack them.

One supplier told investors that its yield rate on the newest stacks has improved from roughly 60 to 75 percent this quarter, but warned that a new packaging line in Taiwan had been pushed back to next spring. Engineers are now working around the clock to qualify a second line.

Equipment vendors are watching closely. More capacity is in the pipeline, executives said, yet customers want guarantees on delivery dates before they commit to long-term orders. For engineers on the factory floor, that means more pressure to keep tools running and to find the root cause of any stoppage quickly.
